# Proposal Template

## Proposal Summary

QueueSystem is a queue management and service workflow application designed for service counters, staff operations, and queue display management.

## Customer Requirements

- organisation name
- deployment model
- number of staff and counters
- queue services required
- domain and hosting environment
- required training and onboarding scope

## Solution Overview

This proposal covers:
- queue management software
- service and counter configuration
- role-based staff access
- queue dashboard and workflow controls
- deployment and support options

## Scope of Work

- software license
- installation and environment setup
- queue configuration and branding
- staff training
- support and maintenance if included

## Pricing

Self-hosted license: AUD $5,000 to $8,000
Managed deployment: AUD $8,000 to $12,000+
Maintenance: AUD $300 to $750 per month

## Delivery Timeline

- discovery review: 1 week
- proposal approval: 2 to 5 business days
- deployment and configuration: depending on environment readiness
- training and handover: on completion of deployment

## Acceptance

The customer will be asked to approve:
- scope of work
- pricing
- deployment plan
- training schedule
- support terms

## Legal note

This proposal should be paired with a signed commercial license and support agreement before deployment or production use.
