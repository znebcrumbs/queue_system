Naming Convention

Use lowercase with underscores: action_resource

Common Actions:
  • view_ - View/List
  • create_ - Create new

  • delete_ - Remove
  • manage_ - Full control

Examples:

  • export_tickets
  • manage_departments
  • reset_passwords
