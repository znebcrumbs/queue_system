# Commercial License Agreement (Template)

## Purpose

This document is a working commercial license template for customer use of QueueSystem. It is not a substitute for a lawyer-reviewed Australian commercial contract.

## Grant of license

The supplier grants the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the software for the licensed environment described in the agreement.

## Permitted use

The customer may:
- install the software in the licensed deployment environment
- operate the software for internal business use
- use the software in the number of organisations, locations, or user counts expressly stated in the agreement

## Restrictions

The customer may not:
- redistribute the source code
- publish or disclose the source code
- resell the software or sublicense it without written approval
- claim ownership of the software or its associated intellectual property
- modify the software beyond the rights expressly permitted in writing

## Ownership

All intellectual property and copyright remain owned by the supplier unless otherwise stated in writing.

## Term and renewal

The license term should be specified in the signed agreement, including:
- initial term
- renewal conditions
- automatic renewal clauses if any
- termination triggers

## Support and maintenance

Support or maintenance is only included where specified in the signed support agreement or maintenance schedule.

## Warranty and liability

The agreement should address:
- warranties
- service levels
- indemnity limitations
- liability caps
- exclusion of consequential damages

## Confidentiality

The customer and supplier should agree to protect confidential information, including deployment configurations, support data, and customer operational details.

## Governing law

For Australian customers, the agreement should specify Australian law and the appropriate jurisdiction for dispute resolution.

## Legal note

This template must be reviewed by a qualified Australian commercial lawyer before use in a binding customer agreement.
