# Customer Support Policy

## Support model

Support is provided under the agreed support and maintenance agreement.

## Response times

The customer support policy should define:
- critical incidents
- urgent operational outages
- non-critical fix requests
- business-hours support windows

## Responsibilities

### Supplier responsibilities
- provide issue triage and support under the maintenance agreement
- coordinate software fixes and updates
- provide deployment and rollback guidance

### Customer responsibilities
- maintain infrastructure access and networking
- maintain secure environment variables
- ensure database backups are performed
- notify the supplier of production problems promptly

## Escalation

Escalation should follow the customer support contact process and include:
- issue summary
- affected service or environment
- operational impact
- timeline and current status

## Maintenance schedule

The maintenance schedule should be documented in writing and aligned with the signed support agreement.
