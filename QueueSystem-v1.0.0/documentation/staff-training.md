# Staff Training

## Objectives

This session ensures staff can correctly manage the queue, call customers, and handle operational exceptions without disrupting service flow.

## Core tasks

### Login and start of day
- log in with assigned credentials
- review assigned service and counters
- confirm the queue is active

### Calling tickets
- identify the next customer in queue
- call the correct ticket
- guide the customer to the correct service point

### Queue controls
- recall a ticket
- skip a ticket when approved
- hold a ticket while waiting for additional work
- mark the ticket complete

### Handling exceptions
- service unavailable
- customer requests a different queue
- customer does not respond
- duplicate or incorrect ticket call

## Safety expectations

- do not bypass the official queue workflow
- do not call tickets for another counter without approval
- escalate issues that may impact customer flow or security

## Training record

The trainer should record the date, trainer name, and attendance list for the session.
