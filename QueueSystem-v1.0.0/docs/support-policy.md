# Support Policy

## Support channels

Customer support is provided by email or the agreed customer support contact channel.

## Support hours

Support hours should be defined in the commercial support agreement and may vary by customer. Standard support should be stated in Australian Eastern Standard Time unless otherwise agreed.

## Response expectations

The following response times should be stated in the customer support contract:

- critical production outage: priority response window
- severe operational failure: same-day acknowledgement
- non-critical issues: next business day acknowledgement

## Maintenance responsibilities

The provider is responsible for:

- security updates when provided under the maintenance agreement
- bug-fix releases under the agreed scope
- support and troubleshooting guidance
- version and update coordination

The customer is responsible for:

- maintaining infrastructure access
- maintaining backups
- maintaining secure environment configuration
- notifying the provider of operational issues promptly

## Incident escalation

For serious incidents:

1. confirm outage scope and impact
2. assess database and service health
3. restore service using the approved rollback or backup process
4. document the incident for follow-up
5. issue the customer update and remediation steps

## Support scope

Support covers the queue application within the agreed deployment scope and standard maintenance terms. Custom integrations, hardware support, and non-standard configuration work are outside the included scope unless separately specified in writing.
